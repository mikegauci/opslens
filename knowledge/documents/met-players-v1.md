# Distinct active settled players

ID: MET-PLAYERS
Version: 1
Effective: 2026-11-08
Scope: metrics
Status: current
Source: knowledge/documents/met-players-v1.md

An active settled player is a synthetic player ID with at least one eligible settled transaction. The Overview counts distinct IDs after applying the requested interval and dimension filters.

A player can appear under two providers and on several days. Adding provider counts or daily counts can double-count that player. Ask for the distinct count over the combined scope if the investigation needs a unique player total.
