Archived portfolio draft superseded by RUN-COMPARE v2 effective 8 November 2026. Its illustrative rule was to compare the current selection against the entire previous calendar week, even if the selection lasted fewer days. It also allowed a signed percentage when the previous GGR was negative.

This draft is retained as conflicting historical evidence. Its guidance was never deployed and must be identified as superseded when explaining current OpsLens comparisons.
