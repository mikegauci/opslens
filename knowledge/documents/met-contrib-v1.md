# Provider contributions to a GGR change

ID: MET-CONTRIB
Version: 1
Effective: 2026-11-08
Scope: metrics
Status: current
Source: knowledge/documents/met-contrib-v1.md

For each provider, subtract its previous-period GGR from its current-period GGR. Include providers that occur in only one period and use zero GGR for their absent side. Signed provider differences sum to the overall GGR change when all use identical filters and periods.

Use absolute EUR amounts with a sign to rank the largest increases or decreases. Opposing signs can cancel in the total and make percentage shares misleading. These differences show where a change was recorded. They cannot establish why it happened.
