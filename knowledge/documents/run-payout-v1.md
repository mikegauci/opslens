# Investigate a payout-led revenue drop

ID: RUN-PAYOUT
Version: 1
Effective: 2026-11-08
Scope: runbooks
Status: current
Source: knowledge/documents/run-payout-v1.md

Verify dataset, filters and periods, then rank providers by signed GGR difference. Compare the largest negative contributor's settled stakes and payouts across both periods. Higher payouts with similar stakes support a payout-led description.

Open the provider contribution's matching Activity selection and inspect the highest-payout settled rows. Record exact scoped aggregates before drawing a conclusion. The documents explain the investigation and its limits. Use analytics results for the figures.
